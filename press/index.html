The game

Play No-Limit Texas Hold’em at casual tables, climb the ranked ladder, enter the weekly tournament and customize your character’s hair and outfits.

Free to download, with optional in-app purchases including virtual chips. No in-game ads. Intended for adults 18+. Chips have no cash value; there is no real-money gambling or cash-out.

Why we made Minamo

We love poker, but felt existing apps often lacked the charm of playing cards and had clunky interfaces, ads and frequent pop-ups. We wanted that card-table personality in a cleaner experience.
